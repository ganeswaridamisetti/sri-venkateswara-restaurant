# Sri Venkateswara Restaurant Website

A full responsive restaurant website built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Pages
- `index.html` — home, highlights, featured dishes, reviews, map
- `about.html` — story and amenities
- `menu.html` — full sample menu with filters, cart and checkout
- `gallery.html` — photo gallery
- `contact.html` — address, hours, map and table request form
- `styles.css` — dark-green and gold design
- `script.js` — menu, cart (saved in the browser), checkout and reservations

## Run locally
1. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static server.
2. Internet access is needed for Google Fonts, Unsplash photos and the map embed.

## Important before publishing
- Menu dishes, prices and photos are sample placeholders, not a confirmed restaurant menu.
- The public listing cited 4.2/5 from 25 reviews and ₹200–₹800 per person. Confirm before publishing.
- Hours on the contact page are typical dining hours, not an official schedule.
- Cart, checkout and reservations work in the browser only. They do not email the restaurant, store customer data on a server, or process payments.
- Replace external photos with restaurant-owned or licensed images before commercial use.
