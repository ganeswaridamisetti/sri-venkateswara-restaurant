WHO WE ARE

Comfort food,
cooked with care.

We serve Andhra and Indian dhaba favourites in Kellampalli’s Pedavullagallu village. Guests know us for Kajju chicken, biryani, meals and a relaxed hall that works for families as well as people passing through.

The restaurant listing rates us 4.2 out of 5 from 25 Google reviews, with a typical spend of ₹200–₹800 per person. We keep the kitchen simple: hot food, fair portions, and space to sit together.
